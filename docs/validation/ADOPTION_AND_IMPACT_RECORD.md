# Adoption and impact record

Adoption and impact are separate claims. An organization can use CascadeLens without proving that it improved an outcome.

## Verified organizational adoption

Required fields are an identifiable organization, permission to publish its name, dated start and end or ongoing status, named task and user group, product release used, evidence of repeated or decision-linked use, and limitations. A public URL, repository clone, page view, star, demo, or maintainer-operated analysis is not adoption.

## Demonstrated real-world impact

In addition to adoption evidence, the record must define the outcome, baseline or counterfactual method, measurement window, data source, missingness, alternative explanations, uncertainty, adverse or null results, and limits on causal wording. A testimonial or before-after number without a defensible comparison is not sufficient.

## Publication

Remove credentials, personal data, and restricted operational details. Preserve a stable public report and exact hash. If the counterparty permits only anonymous publication, the record may describe a pilot but must not increment the public organizational-adoption count because identity cannot be verified publicly.
